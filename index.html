Research

My research develops statistical foundations for deployed AI systems: how to monitor, evaluate, and make decisions about AI agents that are already running in the world. Current threads include anytime-valid sequential monitoring of deployed agent risk and portfolio design for agentic workflows under imperfect selection. My training is in computer science, applied math, AI/ML, econometrics and semiparametric statistics.

Teaching

I have taught courses and guest lectures in computer science, applied mathematics, and economics, covering machine learning and deep learning, machine learning for econometrics, statistical learning, and generative and agentic AI.
